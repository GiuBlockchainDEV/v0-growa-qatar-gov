## Positioning

**Growa** is a sovereign agricultural operations platform. **Growa Qatar** is its first national deployment — a digital command centre for food security, resource accountability, and institutional governance.

> *How can authorized institutions see the full agricultural picture, act together, and protect national food security — without compromising data sovereignty?*

Growa answers that question.

---

## Value at a Glance

> [!card] Spatial Intelligence
> Every metric connects to geography. The map is the operating surface, not an accessory.

> [!card] Resource Accountability
> Water and energy intensity measured per crop, per producer, per region — making sustainability visible and actionable.

> [!card] Sovereign Control
> Each country deployment is fully isolated. Data belongs to the deploying state.

> [!card] Grounded Intelligence
> AI briefings cite live platform data. No autonomous decisions. No invented metrics.

> [!card] Bilingual by Design
> English and Arabic with full RTL layout — built for Gulf institutional use.

## Who It Serves

| Stakeholder | Primary Need |
|-------------|--------------|
| Government ministries | National oversight, policy, inspections |
| Inspection teams | Field cases, evidence, corrective actions |
| State food operators | Production and supply visibility |
| Financial institutions | Programme and portfolio monitoring |
| Farm companies | Site operations and resource tracking |

Each organization sees only what it is authorized to see. Collaboration is explicit, auditable, and controlled.

---

## Platform in One Line

**Growa Qatar connects maps, weather, water and energy analysis, food security planning, compliance, supply, and intelligence — so Qatar can protect food security today and plan for tomorrow.**

## Deployment Model

```
Growa Platform          ←  Reusable foundation (country-agnostic)
    └── Country Instance    ←  Sovereign deployment (isolated data)
            └── Organization    ←  Authorized institution (scoped access)
                    └── User + Role    ←  Permissioned workspace
```
